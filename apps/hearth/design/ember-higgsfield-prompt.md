## Usage notes

- Keep the palette locked: amber #D9763A, golden-peach #E3BE8F, cream #FBF8F3, ink #22201C. Any drift toward red or orange-red kills the warmth.
- The face lives on the inner flame, never on the outer body.
- Ember is a companion, not a cheerleader: calm confidence, small smile, no open-mouth grin except in celebration.
- If a render comes back too cartoonish, add "matte clay, soft studio lighting, editorial minimalism" weight and remove any "cute/kawaii" phrasing.
