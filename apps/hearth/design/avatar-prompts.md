# Avatar generation template

Every Hearth avatar is a clay creature bust in Ember's exact style. Swap only
the bracketed parts. Generate at 1:1, then save to `public/avatars/<id>.jpg`
and add the id to `AVATARS` in `src/components/avatar.tsx`.

## Template

> Premium avatar character design in the exact same style as the Ember mascot: a small round clay [CREATURE] creature, soft 3D render in matte clay, [COLOR] body [HEX] with a soft [LIGHT COLOR] [LIGHT HEX] belly and muzzle area, large expressive dark ink-brown eyes #22201C each with a single warm highlight, tiny content smile, soft blush cheeks, [FEATURE e.g. small rounded ears], gentle inner warmth like firelight through frosted glass. The character feels warm, calm, quietly joyful: a companion, not a cartoon mascot. Front-facing bust portrait, head and shoulders only, centered, generous negative space, solid warm cream background #FBF8F3, soft studio lighting, subtle contact shadow. Style: high-end character design for a luxury wellness app, Pixar-grade softness crossed with editorial minimalism. No text, no letters, no props, no outlines, no watermark.

## Rules

- Palette only from the brand book: flame #D9763A, gold-soft #E3BE8F, honey #D9A441, sage #7D8B6F, plum #8F6E8E, plus their soft tints.
- Same face language as Ember: big ink eyes, one highlight, tiny smile, blush.
- Bust portrait, cream background, circular crop in-app.
- No owls (that is their mascot), no props, no text.
